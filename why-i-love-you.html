Why I love you

A growing list, because I keep finding more.

  1. I love that you're the first person I want to tell when something good or bad happens.
  2. I love that even when we're doing absolutely nothing, I'm still happy just because I'm with you.
  3. I love how intelligent you are, but even more than that, I love how curious you are about the world.
  4. I love that you have your own interests, dreams, and ambitions, and that I get to watch you grow into the person you want to become.
  5. I love that being with you feels like being with my best friend.
  6. I love that you make the ordinary parts of life feel like something worth remembering.
  7. I love you for who you are, not just for what you do for me.
  8. I love that no matter what I decide to do or what I want to accomplish, you're always there encouraging me. Even when I doubt myself, knowing that you believe in me means more than you realize.
  9. I love that you're always willing to help me, even when it's inconvenient or you have a million other things going on. You never make me feel like I'm asking for too much, and I appreciate that more than you know.
  10. I love when you rant to me about your day or tell me what's bothering you. I love knowing that I'm someone you feel comfortable talking to, even when things aren't going great. I never want you to feel like you have to keep everything to yourself.
  11. I love when you cook for me. It's such a simple thing, but it means a lot that you take the time to make something for us. I love getting to spend time with you in the kitchen, and obviously getting to eat whatever you make.
  12. I love that you put so much thought and effort into the things you do for me. Whether it's something big or something small, you always find ways to show me that you care. I notice those things, even when I don't always say it.
